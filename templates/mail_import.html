Import mail

Mail files are imported into one of your accounts and become searchable like synced mail. Supported: Outlook PST/OST, mbox (Thunderbird, Google Takeout, Apple Mail export), Maildir / Maildir++ folders, single .eml files or a folder of them, Apple .emlx, Outlook .msg, and zip / tar archives of any of these. The type is recognised from the content, not the file name. Only messages that are not already in the account (by Message-ID) are added; imported mail is marked read and lands under PST/ or Imported/<type>/.

Archive of imported files

Imported files are kept in each account's done/ folder. Only a dated series (<name>-YYYYMMDD.<ext>, e.g. a nightly export) is ever thinned out, and only right after a successful import of a new copy of that series: the newest {{ cfg.keep_recent }} copies and the newest copy of each of {{ cfg.keep_monthly }} earlier months are kept, never fewer than {{ cfg.min_keep }} copies, never the newest one, at most {{ cfg.max_delete_per_run }} deletions per run. A failed or partial import, or no import at all, deletes nothing. One-off files are kept until you remove them.
